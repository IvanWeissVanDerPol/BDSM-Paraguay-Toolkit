# Sexpo Paraguay — Business Plan

## Brand Identity

- **Name**: Sexpo Paraguay
- **Handle**: @sexpo.paraguay
- **Role in ecosystem**: Awareness engine — large-scale expos that generate media coverage, attract newcomers, normalize adult wellness, and feed the ecosystem
- **History**: Paraguay's first-ever sex expo, launched November 3-4, 2022 at Bar Barbarella. Second edition July 11, 2024 at Tango Asuncion.
- **Tagline proposal**: "Educacion, placer y libertad — sin tabues" (Education, pleasure, and freedom — without taboos)

---

## 1. Vision & Mission

**Vision**: Establish Sexpo Paraguay as South America's most innovative adult wellness expo — a landmark annual event that combines education, entertainment, and commerce while shifting cultural attitudes toward sexual health and freedom.

**Mission**: Produce large-scale events that educate, entertain, and connect — breaking taboos through professional programming, world-class performers, and inclusive spaces that welcome everyone from the curious to the experienced.

## 2. Event History & Lessons Learned

### 2.1 Edition 1 — November 2022

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| **Dates** | November 3-4, 2022 |
| **Venue** | Bar Barbarella, Calle Herrera, Asuncion |
| **Ticket range** | Gs. 70,000 - 300,000 |
| **Programming** | Pregnancy sexuality, OnlyFans workshop, BDSM/Petplay demos, bondage courses, latex fashion, pole dance |
| **Media coverage** | 3 outlets (Extra, Popular, DelParaguay) — all tabloid/entertainment, no mainstream |
| **Controversy** | "Orgy" concerns, conservative celebrity criticism, religious pushback |
| **Key learning** | First-mover advantage established; controversy = free marketing; need better PR strategy |

### 2.2 Edition 2 — July 2024

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| **Date** | July 11, 2024 |
| **Venue** | Tango Asuncion (nightclub) |
| **Performers** | Volcano and Minibeast (Latin American performers) |
| **Ticketing** | Tuti.com.py (mainstream platform — normalization signal) |
| **Documentation** | @sheidemian photography, @mocha.xotic model |
| **Key learning** | Venue upgrade successful; mainstream ticketing platform acceptance = market maturity |

### 2.3 Gap Years

- No evidence of 2023 or 2025 editions
- Suggests resource constraints or strategic regrouping
- Regular cadence (annual or bi-annual) is critical for brand momentum

## 6. Marketing & PR Strategy

### 6.1 Marketing Timeline (6-Month Pre-Event)

| Month | Activity |
|---|---|
| **T-6** | Save the date announcement, early bird tickets |
| **T-5** | Venue reveal, performer announcements begin |
| **T-4** | Workshop lineup published, sponsor acquisition push |
| **T-3** | Full program released, social media campaign ramp-up |
| **T-2** | Media outreach (embargoed previews for journalists), influencer invites |
| **T-1** | Final push: countdown content, last tickets, logistics finalized |
| **Event week** | Location reveal (for new venues), final reminders, backstage content |
| **Post-event** | Media coverage collection, attendee testimonials, save-the-date for next edition |

### 6.2 PR Strategy — Controlling the Narrative

**Lesson from 2022**: Media focus on "orgies" and controversy, not education or empowerment. Strategy must proactively control the narrative.

**Tactics**:
1. **Press kit**: Professional media kit with approved images, key messages, FAQ
2. **Pre-event press conference**: Invite media, present educational focus, introduce speakers
3. **Spokesperson training**: Designated media contacts with talking points
4. **Key messages**:
   - "Sexpo is about education and wellness, not spectacle"
   - "Paraguay deserves the same sexual health discourse as any modern country"
   - "We work with certified sexologists and health professionals"
   - "Consent and safety are our foundation"
5. **Proactive outreach**: Target ABC Color, Ultima Hora (mainstream) not just tabloids
6. **Content creators**: Invite bloggers, podcasters, YouTube creators for authentic coverage
7. **Post-event impact report**: Statistics on HIV tests administered, workshops attended, educational material distributed

### 6.3 Social Media Campaign

**Pre-event (3 months)**:
- Speaker/performer spotlights (2x/week)
- "Why Sexpo matters" testimonial series
- Workshop preview content
- Countdown posts

**Event day**:
- Live stories (backstage, atmosphere — no attendee faces)
- Real-time updates on programming

**Post-event (1 month)**:
- Professional photos (consent-approved)
- Attendee testimonials (anonymized)
- Media coverage roundup
- Impact statistics infographic
- Early announcement for next edition

A smaller, focused event (capacity 150-200) between main Sexpo editions:

- Workshop-heavy
- Local speakers/performers only

## 10. Key Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Venue cancellation (conservative pressure) | Critical | Multiple backup venues, relationship management, deposits |
| Media narrative hijack ("orgy" framing) | High | Proactive PR, press kit, spokesperson training, mainstream media outreach |
| Low sponsorship uptake | Medium | Start with in-kind sponsors, demonstrate ROI with data from each edition |
| Performer cancellation | Medium | Backup performers on standby, strong local talent pipeline |
| Government/police intervention | High | Legal compliance, permits, lawyer on call, no illegal activities |
| Poor attendance | Medium | Aggressive marketing, early bird pricing, influencer push, Maskarada community as base |
| Safety incident | Critical | DM team, medical on-site, crisis protocol, insurance |
| Conservative organized protest | Medium | Security perimeter, media response plan, do not engage |
