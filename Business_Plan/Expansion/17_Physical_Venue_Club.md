# Physical Venue / Permanent Club

## Expansion Business Area #10 | Phase 3 (Year 3) | Priority: MEDIUM

---

## 1. Business Concept

**What**: A dedicated permanent venue serving as the headquarters for all three brands — event space, retail showroom, therapy rooms, content studio, and community gathering space. Operates as a private members' club with controlled access.

**Target Market**: Maskarada members (primary), Fun4Me VIP customers, Academy students, therapists, content creators, third-party event renters.

**Value Proposition**: Eliminates ongoing venue rental costs (Gs. 93M+/year), creates a permanent community home, enables daily operations (not just event nights), and unlocks new revenue streams (bar, retail, studio rental, room rental).

## 3. Location Strategy

### 3.1 Ideal Location Criteria

| Criterion | Requirement | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Zone** | Commercial or mixed-use (NOT residential) | Avoid neighbor complaints, zoning issues |
| **Access** | Main road, parking nearby, taxi/ride-share accessible | Member convenience |
| **Visibility** | Low external visibility preferred | Discretion; members-only, not walk-in |
| **Noise** | Soundproofing possible; no shared walls with residences | Event noise management |
| **Size** | 380-465 m² | Space requirements above |
| **Condition** | Suitable for renovation; basic infrastructure in place | Reduce build-out costs |

### 3.2 Target Neighborhoods (Asuncion)

| Area | Pros | Cons | Est. Rent (Gs./month) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zona Industrial (Mariano Roque Alonso)** | Low rent, warehouse spaces, no neighbors | Far from center, less prestigious | 5,000,000-8,000,000 |
| **Villa Morra / Carmelitas (commercial street)** | Central, accessible, professional | Higher rent, more visibility | 12,000,000-18,000,000 |
| **Centro (near Palma)** | Central, cultural zone | Old buildings, parking issues | 8,000,000-12,000,000 |
| **Fernando de la Mora / San Lorenzo** | Moderate rent, growing commercial areas | Slightly suburban | 6,000,000-10,000,000 |

**Recommended**: Industrial/commercial zone (lower rent, privacy, space) — similar to international precedents (Torture Garden warehouse, Berlin Kitkat Club industrial area).

## 7. Risk Assessment

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zoning/permit denial** | Medium | Very High | Research zoning before signing lease; consult municipal office; choose commercial zone |
| **Neighbor complaints** | Medium | High | Soundproofing; industrial zone; discreet signage; limited external hours |
| **High fixed costs / vacancy** | Medium | High | Multiple revenue streams; aggressive booking; third-party rentals; flexible lease terms |
| **Security incidents** | Low | High | Professional security; access control; vetting; CCTV in common areas; DMs at events |
| **Lease increases** | Medium | Medium | Negotiate long-term lease (3-5 years); option to purchase clause |
| **Capital shortfall** | Medium | Very High | Phase construction; launch with main hall only; add rooms over time; seek investor |
