# Fashion & Apparel Line — "Maskarada Wear"

## Expansion Business Area #6 | Phase 2 (Year 2) | Priority: MEDIUM

---

## 1. Business Concept

**What**: Custom lingerie, fetish wear, lifestyle clothing, and signature accessories brand. Starts with branded merchandise (t-shirts, masks, accessories) and expands to original designs crafted by local Paraguayan artisans and seamstresses.

**Brand**: "Maskarada Wear" — lifestyle fashion sub-brand of Maskarada.

**Target Market**: Maskarada community members, Sexpo attendees, fashion-forward adults, couples seeking special occasion wear, LGBTQ+ community.

**Value Proposition**: Locally designed and crafted fetish/lifestyle wear at accessible prices. Supports local artisans. Unique designs inspired by Paraguayan culture (nanduti lace patterns, guarani motifs). No local competition in kink fashion.

## 7. Risk Assessment

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low demand for premium fetish wear | Medium | Medium | Start with lower-price merchandise; test demand before investing in premium |
| Quality inconsistency from artisans | Medium | High | Quality checks before shipping; clear specifications; backup artisan roster |
| Sizing issues (intimate wear) | High | Medium | Offer made-to-measure; provide detailed size guides; generous exchange policy |
| Competition from cheap imports | Medium | Low | Differentiate on local craftsmanship, customization, brand community; "hecho en Paraguay" |
