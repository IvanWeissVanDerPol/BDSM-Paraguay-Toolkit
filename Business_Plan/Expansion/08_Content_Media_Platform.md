# Content & Media Platform — "Sin Tabues PY"

## Expansion Business Area #1 | Phase 1 (Months 6-12) | Priority: HIGH

---

## 1. Business Concept

**What**: Multi-channel content brand producing educational and entertainment content in Spanish for the LatAm adult wellness market. Anchored by a podcast, supported by YouTube, blog, and social media content.

**Brand**: "Sin Tabues PY" (Without Taboos PY)

**Target Market**: Spanish-speaking adults 22-45 interested in sexuality, relationships, kink education, and sexual wellness. Primary: Paraguay (2.5M Asuncion metro). Secondary: Argentina, Uruguay, Chile, Colombia (total LatAm Spanish-speaking addressable: 200M+).

**Value Proposition**: The only Paraguayan-produced sexual wellness content brand. First-mover advantage in a market with zero local competition in this niche. Combines education, entertainment, and community — in Paraguayan Spanish with local cultural context.

**Content Channels**:

| Channel | Format | Frequency | Audience Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Podcast** ("Sin Tabues PY") | Audio (Spotify, Apple, Google) | Bi-weekly (2x/month) | Primary channel |
| **YouTube** | Video versions of podcast + tutorials | Weekly | Discovery + SEO |
| **Blog** (on fun4me.com.py) | Written articles, guides, SEO content | 2-4 posts/month | SEO + product conversion |
| **Instagram** | Reels, carousels, stories | Daily | Engagement + brand awareness |
| **TikTok** | Short-form educational content | 3-5x/week | Youth audience acquisition |
| **Newsletter** | Email digest + exclusive content | Bi-weekly | Retention + conversion |

## 7. Risk Assessment

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube/Instagram content moderation/demonetization | High | Medium | Focus on Spotify/Apple Podcasts (audio-only, less censorship); use euphemisms in titles |
| Slow audience growth | Medium | Medium | Cross-promote via existing Maskarada/Sexpo audiences (1,000+ existing community); guest appearances on other podcasts |
| Time commitment for founders | Medium | High | Batch-record episodes; outsource editing; set fixed schedule (4-6 hours/week max) |
| Sponsor acquisition difficulty | Medium | Medium | Start with Fun4Me as internal sponsor; approach regional brands after 500+ downloads/episode |
| Cultural backlash / controversy | Low | High | Education-forward positioning; professional tone; prepared crisis response; avoid inflammatory topics |
