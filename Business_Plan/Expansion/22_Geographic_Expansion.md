# Geographic Expansion Strategy

## Expansion Business Area #15 | Phase 2-4 (Year 2+) | Priority: MEDIUM

## 1. Business Concept

**What**: Systematic geographic expansion of the ecosystem — first within Paraguay (Ciudad del Este, Encarnacion), then to Mercosur neighbors (Buenos Aires, Montevideo), and eventually broader Latin America through a franchise/licensing model.

## 3. Franchise Model

### 3.1 Franchise Structure

| Element | Terms |
|---|---|
| **Initial franchise fee** | $5,000 USD (Gs. 37,000,000) |
| **Annual royalty** | 8% of gross revenue |
| **Marketing contribution** | 2% of gross revenue |
| **Territory exclusivity** | Metro area (e.g., Buenos Aires metro) |
| **Contract duration** | 3 years, renewable |
| **Training requirement** | 40 hours (our Instructor Training Program) |
| **Quality standards** | Mandatory safety protocols, brand guidelines, annual audit |

### 3.2 Franchise Package Includes

- Complete brand toolkit (logos, templates, guidelines)
- Event production playbook (50+ documents)
- Safety and consent protocols
- DM training and certification program
- Marketing strategy and content calendar
- Supply chain connections (if applicable)
- 40 hours initial training
- Quarterly review calls
- Access to document updates
- Guest speaker/educator exchange
- Cross-promotion across network

## 5. Risk Assessment

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local partner mismanagement | Medium | High | Contractual quality standards; on-site representative; right to revoke license |
| Regulatory differences by country | High | Medium | Local legal counsel in each market; adapt documents per jurisdiction |
| Brand dilution from poor licensed events | Medium | High | Mandatory training; brand audit; attendee satisfaction surveys; mystery attendees |
| Travel/coordination costs exceed projections | Medium | Medium | Use video conferencing where possible; batch trips; local partners handle most operations |
| Currency fluctuation (multiple currencies) | High | Medium | Price licenses in USD; adjust annually |
