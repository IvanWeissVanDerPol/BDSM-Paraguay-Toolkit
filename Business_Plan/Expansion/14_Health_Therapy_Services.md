# Health & Therapy Services

## Expansion Business Area #7 | Phase 2 (Year 2) | Priority: MEDIUM

---

## 1. Business Concept

**What**: A referral network and co-located therapy service connecting community members with sexologists, couples therapists, and mental health professionals specializing in sexual wellness. Partnership with ECIS (Instituto de Educacion y Ciencias Sexuales).

**Model**: Start as referral/commission model (low investment), transition to co-located services in permanent venue (Phase 3).

**Target Market**: Maskarada community members, Fun4Me customers, corporate wellness referrals, general public seeking sex-positive therapy.

**Value Proposition**: Vetted, kink-aware, sex-positive therapists — a rarity in Paraguay's conservative therapy landscape. Discrete, affordable, accessible (including telehealth).

## 5. Therapist Network

### 5.1 Therapist Requirements

| Criteria | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Education** | Licensed psychologist, psychiatrist, or ECIS-certified sexologist |
| **Specialization** | Sexual health, relationship therapy, or kink-aware practice |
| **Kink-awareness** | Must complete 4-hour kink-awareness training (provided by us) |
| **Insurance** | Must carry professional liability insurance |
| **Availability** | Minimum 10 hours/week for referrals |
| **Telehealth capability** | Must offer WhatsApp video or similar |

### 5.2 ECIS Partnership Structure

| Element | Details |
|---|---|
| **ECIS provides** | Certified sexologists, clinical supervision, curriculum review, institutional credibility |
| **We provide** | Client referrals, booking system, marketing, space (in permanent venue), community access |
| **Revenue split** | 80% to therapist / 20% to ecosystem |
| **Exclusivity** | Non-exclusive; therapists maintain private practices |
| **Training** | ECIS provides clinical training; we provide kink-awareness training |

## 7. Risk Assessment

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Therapist quality / ethical violations | Low | Very High | Vetting process, supervision, client feedback, incident reporting system |
| Client confidentiality breaches | Low | Very High | Encrypted booking system, NDAs with therapists, Ley 6534/2020 compliance |
| Low initial demand | Medium | Medium | Free first sessions at events; partner with Maskarada for member benefits; word-of-mouth |
| ECIS partnership changes | Low | High | Diversify therapist sources; build direct relationships with individual professionals |
| Legal liability | Low | High | Professional liability insurance; clear referral (not employment) relationship; disclaimers |
