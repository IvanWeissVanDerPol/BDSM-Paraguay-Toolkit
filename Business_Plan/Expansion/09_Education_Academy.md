# Education Academy — "Academia Fun4Me"

## Expansion Business Area #2 | Phase 2 (Year 2) | Priority: HIGH

---

## 1. Business Concept

**What**: A structured online and in-person education platform offering courses, certifications, and instructor training in sexual wellness, kink education, consent, and relationship skills.

**Brand**: "Academia Fun4Me" — operated under the Fun4Me Store umbrella with its own landing page and LMS.

**Target Market**:
- Primary: Maskarada members seeking skill development (250+ by Year 3)
- Secondary: General public interested in sexual education (Paraguay + LatAm)
- Tertiary: Aspiring educators and facilitators wanting certification

**Value Proposition**: The only structured, certified kink and sexual wellness education program in Paraguay. Combines online accessibility with in-person workshops. Partnership with ECIS provides clinical credibility.

## 6. Risk Assessment

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low enrollment | Medium | High | Bundle with Maskarada membership; offer free intro courses; cross-promote at all events |
| Instructor quality inconsistency | Medium | High | Rigorous vetting + Training Program; student feedback loops; mystery student audits |
| Platform technical issues | Low | Medium | Use established LMS (Teachable); maintain backups; offer alternative delivery (email, WhatsApp) |
| Competition from free content | Medium | Medium | Differentiate with certification, community access, in-person components, ECIS credibility |
| ECIS partnership breakdown | Low | High | Diversify instructors; develop internal expertise; maintain multiple clinical partnerships |
