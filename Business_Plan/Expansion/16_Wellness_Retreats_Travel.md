# Wellness Retreats & Travel

## Expansion Business Area #9 | Phase 2 (Year 2) | Priority: MEDIUM

---

## 1. Business Concept

**What**: Multi-day immersive experiences at quintas (country estates) and rural venues combining kink education, wellness activities (yoga, massage, meditation), community bonding, and exploration. Also packages for inbound "kink tourism" from Argentina and Brazil.

**Target Market**: Maskarada members seeking deeper experiences, couples, international visitors from Buenos Aires/Sao Paulo, wellness-oriented adults.

**Value Proposition**: Immersive, all-inclusive weekend experiences that combine sexual wellness education with traditional wellness practices — in beautiful Paraguayan country settings. No equivalent offering exists in Paraguay or the broader Mercosur region.

### 3.2 Venue Requirements

- Minimum 30 min from city center (privacy, immersion)
- Private/fenced property (no neighbors within view)
- Sleeping capacity for all attendees + staff
- Kitchen for group meals (or catering access)
- Indoor space for workshops (rain backup)
- Outdoor space for activities
- Pool preferred (especially for summer retreats)
- Good road access (or transport provided)

## 7. Risk Assessment

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weather disruption (outdoor activities) | Medium | Medium | Indoor backup plans; covered areas; flexible scheduling |
| Venue cancellation | Low | High | Contracts with 3+ backup venues; 50% deposit required from venue |
| Group dynamics / conflicts | Medium | Medium | Trained facilitators; consent system (tokens); buddy system; DMs present |
| Low attendance | Medium | High | Minimum attendance policy (cancel if <12); early-bird pricing; Maskarada member priority |
| Medical emergency at remote location | Low | High | First aid trained staff; emergency contact list; vehicle always available; insurance |
