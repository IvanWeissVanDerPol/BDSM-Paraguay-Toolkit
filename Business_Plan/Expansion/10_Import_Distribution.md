# Import & Distribution Business

## Expansion Business Area #3 | Phase 1-2 (Months 6-24) | Priority: HIGH

---

## 1. Business Concept

**What**: Become Paraguay's primary wholesale importer and distributor of adult wellness products. Currently 8-10 online retailers in Paraguay all import individually and inefficiently. Fun4Me can consolidate purchasing power, import in bulk, and distribute to retailers, pharmacies, hotels, and gift shops.

**Target Market**:
- B2B: Paraguayan adult retailers (8-10 known competitors), pharmacies, hotels/motels, gift shops
- B2C: Fun4Me's own retail channel (improved COGS through bulk purchasing)
- Regional: Bolivia and interior Paraguay (Phase 3)

**Value Proposition**: Lower per-unit costs through bulk purchasing, reliable supply chain, private label options, and professional wholesale service in a market with no existing distributor.

---

## 2. Phased Approach

### Phase 1: Dropship/Broker Model (Months 6-18)

**Strategy**: Take wholesale orders from retailers, consolidate orders, import per-batch, deliver. No warehouse needed. Low capital, low risk.

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Investment | Gs. 5,000,000 |
| Model | Order aggregation + per-batch import |
| Minimum order | Gs. 500,000 per retailer per batch |
| Import cycle | Monthly batches from China/Brazil |
| Delivery | Direct to retailer from customs |

### Phase 2: Warehouse Model (Month 19+, when 10+ clients)

**Strategy**: Maintain inventory in bonded warehouse, offer same-day/next-day delivery, stock popular SKUs.

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Additional investment | Gs. 35,000,000 |
| Warehouse | 50-100 m², commercial zone, Asuncion |
| Staff | 1 warehouse manager + 1 delivery |
| Inventory | 200+ SKUs, 500+ units |
| Delivery | Same-day Asuncion, 48h interior |

## 4. Supply Chain

### 4.1 Sourcing Strategy

| Source | % of Catalog | Lead Time | Payment Terms | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **China (Alibaba/1688)** | 60% | 45-60 days | T/T 30% deposit, 70% on shipment | Toys, accessories, rope, BDSM gear |
| **Brazil (Sao Paulo)** | 25% | 7-15 days | Net 30-60 | Lubricants, condoms, lingerie, cosmetics |
| **Argentina** | 10% | 7-15 days | Net 30 | Specialty items, latex, leather |
| **Local Paraguay** | 5% | 1-3 days | Net 15 | Custom items, packaging, artisan products |

### 4.2 Import Process

| Step | Details | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Sourcing & ordering | Negotiate with suppliers, place consolidated orders | — |
| Shipping (China) | Sea freight to Paranagua (Brazil), overland to CDE/ASU | 8-12% of product cost |
| Customs clearance | Customs broker handles declaration, duties, inspections | 5-8% of declared value |
| IVA on imports | 10% VAT on CIF value | 10% |
| Warehousing | Storage until distributed | Gs. 2M/month |
| Distribution | Delivery to retailers | Gs. 500K/month |

### 4.3 Ciudad del Este Free Trade Zone

**Opportunity**: CDE's free trade zone offers reduced import duties. Products imported through CDE can be re-exported to Asuncion at lower cost.

| Benefit | Details |
|---|---|
| Import duty reduction | 0-5% vs. 10-15% regular |
| IVA deferral | Deferred until domestic sale |
| Storage | Lower-cost warehouse options |
| Risk | More complex logistics, regulatory changes |

## 7. Risk Assessment

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customs delays | High | Medium | Build 30-day buffer stock; diversify ports (CDE + ASU) |
| Currency fluctuation (USD/PYG) | High | Medium | Price in USD to B2B clients; hedge with forward contracts |
| Competitor retaliation | Medium | Medium | Offer competitive pricing and superior service; don't undersell drastically |
| Capital locked in inventory | Medium | High | Start with dropship model; stock only fast-movers; 60-day payment terms |
| Regulatory changes on imports | Low | High | Monitor customs regulations; maintain relationships with broker; diversify sources |
