# Event Production Company

## Expansion Business Area #8 | Phase 2 (Year 2) | Priority: MEDIUM

---

## 1. Business Concept

**What**: Leverage Maskarada and Sexpo production expertise to produce events for third parties — bachelorette/bachelor parties, private celebrations, corporate events, LGBTQ+ events, nightclub promotions, and licensed event formats.

**Target Market**: Event planners, nightclubs, corporate HR, LGBTQ+ organizations, individuals planning celebrations, other adult wellness communities.

**Value Proposition**: Professional event production with unique expertise in themed adult events, consent culture, safety protocols, and community management. The only production company in Paraguay with this specialization.

## 6. Risk Assessment

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Liability during client events | Medium | High | Comprehensive event insurance; signed waivers; professional DMs at all events |
| Seasonal demand fluctuation | High | Medium | December and February peak (holidays, Valentine's); off-peak pricing; corporate events year-round |
| Client dissatisfaction | Low | High | Detailed contracts; pre-event walkthrough; post-event feedback; refund policy |
| Staff availability | Medium | Medium | Maintain roster of 15+ trained event staff; pay competitive rates |
| Reputation risk from third-party events | Medium | High | Selective client vetting; brand guidelines for licensed events; right to refuse |
