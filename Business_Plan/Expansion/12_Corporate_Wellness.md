# Corporate Wellness Division — "Bienestar Sexual PY"

## Expansion Business Area #5 | Phase 1 (Months 6-12) | Priority: HIGH

---

## 1. Business Concept

**What**: Offer professional sexual health education workshops to corporations, universities, and NGOs. Positioned as mainstream "workplace wellness" and "health education" — completely separate branding from the kink/adult entertainment ecosystem.

**Brand**: "Bienestar Sexual PY" — separate website, separate social media, professional healthcare positioning. No visible connection to Fun4Me, Maskarada, or Sexpo branding.

**Target Market**:
- Corporations: HR departments, compliance teams, DEI initiatives
- Universities: Student wellness programs, psychology/sociology departments
- NGOs: LGBTQ+ organizations, women's rights groups, health NGOs
- Government: Ministry of Health programs, public health campaigns

**Value Proposition**: Professional, evidence-based sexual health education facilitated by certified sexologists (ECIS partnership). Fills a gap in Paraguay's corporate wellness market where no provider currently offers this service.

## 7. Risk Assessment

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corporate clients discover kink brand connection | Medium | High | Complete brand separation; different website, social media, phone number; ECIS as face of the brand |
| Conservative corporate culture resists topic | High | Medium | Lead with harassment prevention (legally required); frame as compliance, not sexuality |
| Facilitator availability | Medium | Medium | Train 5+ facilitators through ECIS partnership; maintain backup roster |
| Economic downturn reduces corporate budgets | Medium | Medium | Offer tiered pricing; position as compliance necessity (not optional wellness) |
| Legal liability from workshop content | Low | High | Professional liability insurance; ECIS clinical backing; written disclaimers; participant consent forms |
