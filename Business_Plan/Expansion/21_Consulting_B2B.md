# Consulting & B2B Services

## Expansion Business Area #14 | Phase 4 (Year 3+) | Priority: LOW

---

## 1. Business Concept

**What**: Package the ecosystem's operational documents, safety protocols, event playbooks, and business plans into consulting services for other cities and countries wanting to launch similar adult wellness ecosystems. License the "BDSM Paraguay Toolkit" and the "Sexpo" format.

**Target Market**: Community organizers, event producers, entrepreneurs, and organizations in other LatAm cities wanting to build adult wellness ecosystems.

**Value Proposition**: A battle-tested, fully documented operational framework — 70+ documents covering every aspect from legal structure to event production to safety protocols. No one else in Latin America has this level of documentation and proven execution.

## 7. Risk Assessment

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quality control of licensed events | Medium | High | Detailed brand guidelines; on-site representative at first edition; performance metrics |
| IP theft (clients copy without paying) | Medium | Medium | Contracts with non-compete clauses; trademark registration; unique elements hard to replicate |
| Market too small for consulting | Medium | Low | Consulting is low-cost to operate; even 2-3 clients/year is profitable |
| Reputation damage from bad licensee | Low | High | Right to revoke license; mandatory training; mystery attendee audits |
