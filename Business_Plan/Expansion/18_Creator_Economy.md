# Content Creator Economy

## Expansion Business Area #11 | Phase 3 (Year 3) | Priority: LOW-MEDIUM

---

## 1. Business Concept

**What**: Support Paraguay's emerging adult content creators with studio space, equipment, education, and community. Operate a creator incubator, studio rental service, and affiliate network connecting creators with Fun4Me products.

**Target Market**: OnlyFans/Fansly creators, Instagram influencers, TikTok creators, photographers, videographers in the adult/lifestyle/fashion space.

**Value Proposition**: Professional studio space and mentorship for adult content creators in a market with zero infrastructure for this rapidly growing creator economy.

## 6. Risk Assessment

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legal risks of adult content creation | Medium | High | Legal guidance documents; clear disclaimers; no underage access; Paraguay's permissive legal environment |
| Equipment damage/theft | Medium | Medium | Security deposit required; insurance; inventory tracking |
| Reputational risk | Low | Medium | Vet creators; brand guidelines for affiliate content; right to terminate partnerships |
| Low demand | Medium | Medium | Start small (2 days/week studio access); grow based on demand; multi-use studio space |
