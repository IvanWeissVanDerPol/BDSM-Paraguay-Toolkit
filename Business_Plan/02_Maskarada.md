Maskarada is not just a party — it is a **structured social experience** where:
- Masks and costumes lower social barriers
- Token/game systems create organic interaction
- Themes provide a framework for exploration
- Anonymity enables authenticity in a conservative society
- Consent protocols are embedded in the experience design

| Traditional Party | Maskarada |

| No community between events | Ongoing digital community engagement |

### 3.3 The Token Game System

The token system is Maskarada's signature differentiator — a gamified consent and interaction framework:

**How it works**:

1. **Entry tokens**: Every attendee receives a set of colored tokens upon arrival
2. **Token colors signal interest levels**:
   - Green: "Open to conversation and light interaction"
   - Blue: "Looking to observe and learn"
   - Red: "Open to deeper experiences"
   - Gold: "Available for specific requests" (with written preferences)
3. **Exchange mechanic**: To interact beyond casual conversation, you offer a token to someone. They can accept (and reciprocate with their token) or politely decline (keeping the token as a compliment)
4. **Token rewards**: Collected tokens can be exchanged for:
   - Fun4Me Store discounts
   - Priority access to future events
   - Entry to VIP areas
   - Raffle entries for prizes
5. **Consent is built in**: The physical act of offering/accepting tokens makes consent visible and normalized

**Advanced token variants** (introduced at larger events):
- **Challenge tokens**: Contain a dare or activity suggestion
- **Mystery tokens**: Sealed — reveal a surprise experience when opened with a partner
- **Connection tokens**: Exchange contact info through the system (never directly, maintaining anonymity)

### 4.2 Vetting Process

All new attendees go through a structured vetting process:

1. **Application**: Online form via Google Forms or Telegram bot
   - Basic demographics (age verification required)
   - How they heard about Maskarada
   - Experience level and interests
   - Agreement to community code of conduct
2. **Interview**: Brief voice/video call with a community ambassador
   - Assess genuine interest vs. voyeurism
   - Explain rules, consent protocols, expectations
   - Answer questions
3. **Probation**: First 2 events are "probationary"
   - Assigned a community buddy
   - Limited to Social and Noche events
   - Feedback collected from staff and other attendees
4. **Full membership**: After probation, unlocks all tier benefits

## 5. Safety & Consent Framework

### 5.1 Non-Negotiable Rules

1. **Enthusiastic consent required** for all interactions beyond casual conversation
2. **"No" is complete** — no explanations needed, no persistence allowed
3. **Safe words honored universally**: "Rojo" (stop everything), "Amarillo" (slow down/check in)
4. **No photography** of attendees without explicit written consent
5. **No substances** that impair judgment beyond moderate alcohol
6. **DMs present** at all events — visible, approachable, empowered to intervene
7. **Zero tolerance** for consent violations — immediate removal, permanent ban
8. **Confidentiality**: What happens at Maskarada stays at Maskarada

### 5.2 Incident Response

- Every event has a designated Safety Lead
- Incident report form available digitally
- Post-event debrief within 48 hours
- Community ban list maintained (shared with Sexpo)
- Support resources provided to affected parties
- Detailed protocols in `Investigacion/02_Organization_Toolkit/Seguridad/`

## 6. Marketing Strategy

### 6.1 Growth Model

Maskarada grows through **trust, not advertising**:

```
Sexpo (mass awareness) → Instagram (curiosity) → Social event (first contact)
    → Maskarada Noche (experience) → Membership (commitment) → Referral (growth)
```

### 6.2 Content Strategy (Instagram)

- **Monday**: "Masked Monday" — themed aesthetic photo, caption about upcoming theme
- **Wednesday**: Educational post — consent, kink terminology, scene negotiation
- **Friday**: Event countdown / attendee testimonial (anonymized)
- **Stories daily**: Behind-the-scenes prep, polls, Q&A, theme reveals
- **Reels**: 15-30 second atmospheric videos (no faces, focus on aesthetics)

### 6.3 Referral Engine

- Every Iniciado and Guardian member gets 2 referral invites per quarter
- Referred members skip part of vetting (still do interview)
- Referrer gets bonus tokens at next event
- Tracking via unique referral codes in Telegram bot

### 6.4 Privacy-First Promotion

All marketing follows strict privacy rules:
- No attendee faces in public content (masks, silhouettes, angles only)
- No names or handles shared publicly
- Event photos require individual written release
- Location revealed only 24 hours before event (for vetted attendees)
- Separate "clean" social media presence for algorithm safety

## 10. Key Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Venue cancellation (conservative pressure) | High | 3+ backup venues, relationship with venue owners, deposit protection |
| Safety incident at event | Critical | DM training, insurance, crisis protocol, zero tolerance policy |
| Community drama / internal conflict | Medium | Conflict resolution protocol, mediation team, transparent governance |
| Police/regulatory interference | Medium | Legal counsel on retainer, event permits, no illegal activities |
| Instagram account shutdown | High | Telegram as backup, email list, physical flyer network |
| Slow membership growth | Medium | Lower entry barriers, free trial events, Sexpo funnel |
| Outing/privacy breach | Critical | Strict photography rules, NDAs for staff, digital security practices |
