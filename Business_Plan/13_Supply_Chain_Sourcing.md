## 2. Sourcing by Geography

### 2.1 China — Largest Source for Mass Market Products

**Platforms**:
- **Alibaba** — B2B wholesale, factory direct, MOQ 50-500 units
- **AliExpress** — Small quantities (1-50), higher per-unit cost but no MOQ risk
- **DHgate** — Mid-range wholesale, sometimes better for sex toys
- **1688.com** — Chinese-language Alibaba (lower prices, requires agent)
- **Made-in-China.com** — Alternative B2B platform

**How to source from China**:
1. Search product category on Alibaba/AliExpress
2. Filter by: trade assurance, gold supplier, 4+ years
3. Request samples (pay for samples — expect $5-30 + shipping per sample)
4. Test samples thoroughly: material safety, functionality, packaging quality
5. Negotiate pricing at volume (typical discount: 15-30% at 100+ units)
6. Place trial order (50-100 units per SKU)
7. Use trade assurance or PayPal for payment protection
8. Choose shipping: air freight (7-15 days) or sea freight (45-60 days)

**Key considerations**:
- **Quality control**: ALWAYS order samples first. Many Chinese sellers list body-safe materials but deliver cheap alternatives
- **Material safety**: Insist on body-safe silicone (not TPE/TPR for insertable toys), ABS plastic, stainless steel
- **Customs**: Paraguay customs may inspect packages — use discreet shipping descriptions ("personal massagers", "wellness products")
- **Shipping**: DHL/FedEx for small urgent orders, sea freight via consolidator for bulk
- **Language**: Many suppliers speak English; use Google Translate or hire bilingual assistant for complex negotiations

### 2.2 Brazil — Closest Major Market

**Why Brazil**:
- Mercosur member — preferential tariffs
- Mature adult industry with established distributors
- Proximity — overland shipping via CDE is fast and cheap
- Portuguese/Spanish language barrier is manageable
- Major lubricant and condom brands have Brazilian operations

**Key Brazilian suppliers**:
- **Pjur Brazil** (lubricants) — premium brand, Brazilian distributor
- **Prudence** (condoms) — DKT International, manufactured in Brazil
- **Brazilian lingerie manufacturers** — world-class lingerie industry
- **Erotika Fair exhibitors** — source new products at the annual expo
- **Tantric products** — massage oils, candles, aromatherapy from Brazilian wellness brands

**How to source from Brazil**:
1. Attend Erotika Fair (Sao Paulo, annually) — meet suppliers in person
2. Use Mercosur preferential tariffs (check current rates with customs agent)
3. Ship overland: Sao Paulo → Foz do Iguacu → Ciudad del Este → Asuncion (3-5 days)
4. Consider using a CDE-based import agent (many exist for cross-border trade)
5. Payment: bank wire (international transfer to Brazil) or via import agent

**Why Argentina**:
- Cultural alignment — Argentine suppliers understand our market
- Active kink artisan community (leather workers, rope makers)
- Close proximity — Buenos Aires → Asuncion flights/buses
- Spanish language — no language barrier

**What to source from Argentina**:

- Artisan BDSM furniture — wood and metalworkers in BA
- Shibari rope (jute, processed) — from Argentine riggers

**How to source from Argentina**:

3. Negotiate directly with artisans — consignment or wholesale
4. Ship: bus cargo (cheaper) or courier (faster)

## 3. Import & Customs

### 3.1 Paraguay Import Basics

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Import license** | Required — register as importer with Ministerio de Industria y Comercio |
| **Customs broker** | Required for formal imports — use licensed despachante aduanero |
| **Import duties** | Varies by product category (5-30%) — check current tariff schedule with customs agent |
| **IVA on imports** | 10% on declared value + duties |
| **Mercosur preferential tariff** | Reduced or zero duties for products originating in Brazil, Argentina, Uruguay |
| **Prohibited items** | None specific to adult products found — but check current regulations |
| **Customs valuation** | Based on declared invoice value — under-declaring is illegal and risky |

### 3.2 Product Classification (Suggested Categories)

| Product | Likely Tariff Code Category | Duty Estimate |
|---|---|---|
| Vibrators/massagers | Electrical personal care appliances | 10-20% |
| Textiles (lingerie, costumes) | Clothing/textiles | 20-30% |
| Leather goods | Leather articles | 10-20% |
| Lubricants | Cosmetics/personal care | 5-15% |
| Condoms | Medical/health products | 0-5% |
| Rope | Cordage | 5-10% |
| Furniture | Furniture | 10-20% |
| Printed materials | Books/printed matter | 0-5% |
| Games/cards | Toys/games | 10-15% |

**Important**: Consult with a licensed customs broker for exact tariff classifications and rates before importing.

### 3.3 Import Channels

| Channel | Best For | Cost | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Formal import** (despachante) | Large shipments >$2,000 | Customs fees + broker fee ($200-500) | 1-4 weeks processing |
| **Small import** (régimen simplificado) | Small shipments <$2,000 | Lower fees | 1-2 weeks |
| **Personal carry** (viajero) | Samples, small quantities when traveling | Duty-free allowance up to $500 | Immediate |
| **CDE channel** (buy in Ciudad del Este) | Brazilian products available at CDE traders | No formal import, cash purchase | Same day |
| **Courier** (DHL, FedEx, UPS) | Urgent small shipments | Courier handles customs, higher shipping cost | 3-7 days |
| **Postal** (Correo Paraguayo) | Non-urgent small shipments | Cheapest but slowest, customs may hold | 2-8 weeks |

### 3.4 Discreet Import Practices

| Practice | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Generic product descriptions** on invoices | "Personal care devices", "wellness products", "massage equipment" |
| **Plain packaging** from suppliers | No explicit imagery on outer packaging |
| **Multiple shipments** | Split large orders to avoid attention and reduce per-shipment value |
| **Reputable courier** | DHL/FedEx handle customs professionally, less risk of delays |
| **Documentation** | Keep all import documentation for tax compliance |

## 5. Packaging & Delivery

### 5.1 Discreet Packaging Standard

**Every order must be packaged discreetly**:
- Plain brown or white box/mailer (no branded packaging on outside)
- No product names visible on shipping label
- Return address: "F4M Bienestar" or similar neutral name
- No receipts or invoices visible from outside
- Inside: branded tissue paper or thank-you card (adds premium feel while remaining discreet outside)
- Fragile items wrapped in bubble wrap
- Batteries included where needed (and tested before shipping)

### 5.2 Delivery Options

| Method | Coverage | Speed | Cost (Gs.) | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Moto delivery** (personal or app-based) | Asuncion + Gran Asuncion | Same day or next day | 15,000-30,000 | Urban orders, fast delivery |
| **Bolt/PedidosYa delivery** | Asuncion | 1-3 hours | 15,000-40,000 | Urgent same-day delivery |
| **Encomienda** (bus cargo) | All Paraguay (interior cities) | 1-3 days | 20,000-50,000 | Interior orders, larger packages |
| **Correo Paraguayo** (postal) | All Paraguay + international | 3-10 days | 15,000-40,000 | Budget option, tracking available |
| **Private courier** (Flash, etc.) | Asuncion + major cities | Next day | 25,000-50,000 | Reliable, tracked delivery |
| **Pickup at event** | Event venues | Event day | Gs. 0 | Saves shipping, personal interaction |
| **Pickup point** | Designated location | Arranged | Gs. 0 | Privacy (no home delivery) |

### 5.3 Shipping Cost Strategy

| Option | Description | Customer Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Flat rate** | Fixed shipping fee regardless of order size | Simple, predictable |
| **Free above threshold** | Free shipping on orders above Gs. 200,000 | Increases average order value |
| **Pickup discount** | Discount for event pickup or pickup point | Reduces delivery costs |
| **Subscription free shipping** | Free delivery for subscription box subscribers | Incentivizes subscriptions |
| **Interior surcharge** | Additional fee for delivery outside Gran Asuncion | Covers real costs |

## 6. Quality Assurance

### 6.1 Product Safety Standards

| Material | Safe? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Medical-grade silicone** | Yes | Best for insertable toys, non-porous, hypoallergenic |
| **ABS plastic** | Yes | Hard plastic, body-safe, non-porous |
| **Stainless steel** | Yes | Body-safe, non-porous, heavy |
| **Borosilicate glass** | Yes | Body-safe, non-porous, beautiful |
| **TPE/TPR** | Caution | Porous, cannot be fully sterilized, requires condom use |
| **Jelly rubber** | No | Contains phthalates, toxic — DO NOT SELL |
| **PVC** | Caution | May contain phthalates, sells only with clear warnings |
| **Latex** | Caution | Allergy risk — always disclose, offer alternatives |
| **Genuine leather** | Yes | For external use only, natural material |
| **Vegan leather/PU** | Yes | For external use, affordable alternative |
| **Natural fiber rope** (jute, hemp, cotton) | Yes | For bondage use, body-safe with proper preparation |

### 6.2 Quality Check Procedure

Before listing any product:
1. **Visual inspection** — Check for defects, rough edges, loose parts
2. **Material verification** — Confirm materials match supplier's claims (smell test for silicone vs. cheap rubber)
3. **Functionality test** — Turn on, test all functions, check battery life
4. **Packaging inspection** — Intact packaging, clear instructions, safety information included
5. **Photo documentation** — Product photos for website listing
6. **Safety information** — Create Spanish-language safety card for each product category
7. **Pricing** — Set price based on cost, competitors, and margin targets

### 6.3 Product Returns Policy

| Scenario | Policy |
|---|---|
| **Defective product** (DOA) | Full refund or replacement, return shipping paid by F4M |
| **Wrong product sent** | Full refund or replacement, return shipping paid by F4M |
| **Customer changed mind** (unopened, sealed) | Exchange or store credit within 7 days, customer pays return shipping |
| **Customer changed mind** (opened/used) | No return for hygiene reasons — exception for defects |
| **Damaged in shipping** | Full refund or replacement, insurance claim against courier |

## 8. Supplier Relationship Management

### 8.1 Supplier Database

Maintain a database with:
- Supplier name, contact person, contact info
- Products supplied, pricing history
- Order history (dates, quantities, amounts)
- Quality rating (1-5)
- Reliability rating (1-5)
- Lead time (order to delivery)
- Payment terms
- MOQ requirements
