## How to Build, Grow, and Sustain Audience Across Every Channel

## 1. Marketing Philosophy

### 1.1 Core Principles

1. **Education first, sales second** — Every piece of content should teach something, even promotional content
2. **Algorithm-safe** — Public content must survive platform moderation (no nudity, no explicit language, no graphic imagery)
3. **Community voice** — Feature community members (with consent), not just brand messaging
4. **Consistent but not predictable** — Regular posting cadence with variety in format and topic
5. **Discreet accessibility** — People should be able to follow/engage without outing themselves
6. **Three brands, one strategy** — Coordinated content calendar across Fun4Me, Maskarada, and Sexpo

### 1.2 Content Pillars

| Pillar | What It Covers | % of Content | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Educate** | Workshops, tips, guides, myths debunked, health info, how-tos | 40% | Build trust, SEO, establish authority |
| **Inspire** | Event teasers, community stories, art, fashion, empowerment | 25% | Create desire, emotional connection |
| **Sell** | Product spotlights, promotions, new arrivals, bundles, subscription boxes | 20% | Drive revenue directly |
| **Connect** | Polls, Q&A, discussions, memes, behind-the-scenes, community features | 15% | Build engagement and loyalty |

### 2.2 Language Guidelines

**DO**:
- Use "bienestar sexual" (sexual wellness) instead of "sex shop"
- Use "placer" (pleasure) instead of clinical or vulgar terms
- Use "explorar" (explore) to suggest agency and curiosity
- Use "comunidad" (community) to emphasize belonging
- Use inclusive language: "todas, todos y todes" or neutral constructions
- Use Paraguayan Spanish with occasional guarani touches where authentic

**DON'T**:
- Use vulgar or crude language (even in "edgy" content)
- Use language that implies shame or taboo
- Use medical jargon without explanation
- Use English terminology without Spanish explanation
- Use gendered language when gender-neutral is possible
- Use language that could be seen as promoting illegal activity

### 6.1 Media Targets

| **Digital news** | Ultima Hora, ABC Digital, Hoy, La Nacion | Press releases, offer exclusive interviews, data-driven stories |

| **International media** | AFP, Reuters, BBC Mundo (for major events) | Press releases in English/Spanish for regional interest stories |
| **Niche media** | Feminist blogs, LGBTQ+ media, health publications | Targeted stories aligned with their editorial interests |

### 6.2 Press Kit Contents

Every major event (especially Sexpo) should have a professional press kit:

1. **Press release** — Standard format, AP style, 1-2 pages
2. **Fact sheet** — Key statistics, dates, pricing, capacity
3. **Background document** — Ecosystem overview, mission, impact data
4. **Speaker/performer bios** — Professional headshots and biographies
5. **Approved photos** — High-resolution event photos from past events (consent-cleared)
6. **Spokesperson availability** — Named contact for interviews with phone/WhatsApp
7. **FAQs** — Pre-answered common media questions (including anticipated hostile questions)
8. **Social media assets** — Pre-made graphics for media to share
9. **Video B-roll** — Short video clips media can use in coverage

### 6.3 Crisis Communication Plan

**Pre-prepared statements for common scenarios**:

| Scenario | Key Message |
|---|---|
| **"It's just an orgy"** | "Our events combine education, art, and community. We offer X workshops, partner with Y health organizations, and have served Z people with free STI testing." |
| **"Children could be exposed"** | "All our events are strictly 18+. We verify age at entry with government-issued ID. Child safety is our absolute priority." |
| **Conservative protest** | "We respect all viewpoints. Our focus is on consensual adult wellness, education, and health. We partner with [ECIS, Ministry of Health, etc.]." |
| **Safety incident** | "The safety of our community is our highest priority. We are aware of the incident, are cooperating fully with authorities, and are providing support to all affected parties." |
| **Social media ban** | "We continue to serve our community through [alternative channels]. Our mission of education and wellness continues." |

### 6.4 Media Spokesperson Training

Train 2-3 people on:
- Key talking points (memorized, natural delivery)
- Bridging technique (redirect hostile questions to key messages)
- Sound bite creation (quotable 10-15 second answers)
- Body language and presence on camera
- What NOT to say (avoid speculation, personal opinions, inflammatory language)
- Handling ambush/hostile interviews
