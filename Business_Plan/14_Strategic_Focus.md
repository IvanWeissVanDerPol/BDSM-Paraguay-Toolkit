# Strategic Focus — What We Will (and Will NOT) Do

## The Anti-Plan

> "Strategy is not about what you do. It's about what you deliberately choose NOT to do."

This document exists because the rest of this business plan contains hundreds of ideas, event formats, brand extensions, partnerships, and revenue streams. That's brainstorming, not strategy. **Strategy is choosing 8 things and saying no to 200.**

## 2. Decision Framework

### 2.1 When to Add a New Initiative

Before adding ANY new program, event format, product line, or partnership, answer ALL of these:

| Question | Required Answer |
|----------|----------------|
| Does someone on the team have capacity to own this? | Yes — named person, <30% of their time |
| Does it serve our existing community or attract NEW proven segments? | Yes — with evidence |
| Can we pilot it with <Gs. 2,000,000 ($270) investment? | Yes |
| Does it generate revenue or directly feed revenue-generating activities? | Yes |
| Can we measure success within 90 days? | Yes — with specific metric |
| If it fails, can we shut it down cleanly? | Yes — no long-term commitments |

**If any answer is "no" or "I think so" — DON'T DO IT.**

### 2.2 When to Kill an Initiative

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Event format averages <15 attendees for 3 consecutive editions | Kill or radically redesign |
| Product category generates <Gs. 500,000/month after 6 months | Discontinue |
| Content channel grows <5% month-over-month for 3 months | Reduce investment or kill |
| Partnership generates zero measurable value after 6 months | End partnership |
| Any initiative that requires >40% of one person's time but generates <10% of revenue | Reevaluate or kill |

### 2.3 Quarterly Strategic Review

Every 3 months, the founding team must:
1. List everything they're currently doing
2. Rank by impact (revenue generated or community growth driven)
3. Kill the bottom 20%
4. Ask: "If we could only do 3 things next quarter, which 3?"
5. Allocate 80% of effort to those 3 things

## 4. Saying No — Practical Guide

### 4.1 How to Say No to Good Ideas

"That's a great idea — let's add it to the Year 2 backlog."

### 4.2 How to Say No to Partner Requests

"We'd love to collaborate. Right now we're focused on [our 8 things]. Can we revisit this in Q1 2027?"

### 4.3 How to Say No to Community Feature Requests

"We hear you. We're building the foundation right now and want to get the basics right before adding more. Your idea is noted for when we're ready."

### 4.4 How to Say No to Yourself

If you're considering a new initiative, wait 2 weeks. If you still want to do it after 2 weeks AND it passes the decision framework above, then consider it. Most ideas don't survive 2 weeks of reflection.
