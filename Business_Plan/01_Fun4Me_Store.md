## 3. Sales Channels

### 3.1 E-Commerce Website (Primary — rebuild required)

**Platform options**:
- **WooCommerce on WordPress** — flexible, lower cost, good for Paraguay's payment landscape
- **Shopify** — easier to manage, but limited local payment integration
- **Custom build** — maximum control, higher dev cost

**Key requirements**:
- Age verification gate (18+)
- Discreet billing and packaging options
- WhatsApp integration for customer support
- Integration with local payment methods (Tigo Money, Personal Pay, bank transfers)
- Product education content alongside each listing
- Mobile-first design (80%+ of Paraguayan internet is mobile)
- SEO-optimized product pages (in Spanish)
- Review/rating system with verified purchases

### 3.2 WhatsApp Business (Secondary)

Paraguay's dominant commerce channel. Must support:
- Product catalog via WhatsApp Business API
- Order placement through chat
- Payment confirmation screenshots
- Delivery tracking updates
- Quick replies for common questions

### 3.3 Instagram & TikTok Shopping

### 3.4 Event Pop-up Sales

## 4. Operations

### 4.1 Supply Chain

**Sourcing strategy**:
- **China direct** (Alibaba, 1688): Vibrators, toys, accessories — 60% of catalog
- **Brazil**: Lingerie, latex wear, lubricants — 25% of catalog
- **Argentina**: Rope, leather goods, artisanal items — 10% of catalog
- **Local Paraguay**: Custom furniture, printed materials — 5% of catalog

**Import considerations**:
- Paraguay has relatively open import policies via Ciudad del Este
- No specific import restrictions on adult products (no obscenity customs seizure precedent found)
- Bulk imports through Ciudad del Este free trade zone can reduce costs
- Minimum order quantities: plan for 50-100 units per SKU for initial stock

### 4.2 Inventory Management

- Start with 80-120 SKUs across all categories
- Maintain 2-3 months of inventory for bestsellers
- Pre-order model for high-ticket items (furniture, premium toys)
- Dropship arrangement for slow-moving specialty items
- Track: sell-through rate, days of inventory, reorder points

### 4.3 Fulfillment & Delivery

| Method | Coverage | Timeline | Cost to Customer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Moto delivery (Asuncion)** | Greater Asuncion | Same-day/next-day | Gs. 25,000-40,000 |
| **Express courier** | Interior cities | 2-3 days | Gs. 40,000-80,000 |
| **Bus encomienda** | Rural Paraguay | 3-5 days | Gs. 30,000-60,000 |
| **Event pickup** | At events | Event day | Free |

**Discreet packaging protocol**:
- Plain brown/white boxes with no branding
- Return address: "F4M Comercial" (not Fun4Me Store)
- No product descriptions on external packaging
- Sealed internal packaging
- Option for alternate delivery name

### 4.4 Customer Service

- WhatsApp: Primary support channel (Mon-Sat 9am-9pm)
- Instagram DM: Secondary channel
- Email: For formal inquiries and complaints
- Response time targets: WhatsApp <2 hours, DM <4 hours, Email <24 hours
- FAQ section on website for self-service
- Returns policy: 7-day return for unopened/sealed items, no returns on opened intimate products
