## 6. Founder Agreement

### 6.1 Must-Have Terms (Before Spending Any Money)

- [ ] **Equity split** — Who owns what % of the SAS
- [ ] **Vesting schedule** — Does equity vest over time? (Recommended: 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff)
- [ ] **Decision-making** — How are disagreements resolved? (Majority? Consensus? Specific domains?)
- [ ] **IP assignment** — All work product belongs to the SAS, not individuals
- [ ] **Non-compete** — Can founders work on competing projects?
- [ ] **Exit terms** — What happens if a founder wants to leave? (Buyout formula)
- [ ] **Capital calls** — Can the company require additional investment from founders?
- [ ] **Roles and responsibilities** — Written, not assumed
- [ ] **Compensation** — When do founders start taking salary? (Recommended: only after positive cash flow)

> **WARNING:** More startups die from founder conflicts than from market failure. Get these terms in writing BEFORE the first Guaraní is spent. A 2-hour meeting with a lawyer now prevents a 2-year lawsuit later.
