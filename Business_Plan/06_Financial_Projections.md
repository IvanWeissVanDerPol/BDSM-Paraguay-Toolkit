### 4.1 Key Observations

- **Year 1-2**: Investment phase — team building and market development drive costs above revenue
- **Year 3**: Break-even and first profit achieved
- **Biggest cost driver**: Team salaries (~54% of fixed costs)
- **Biggest revenue lever**: Maskarada memberships and Fun4Me product sales growth
- **Path to faster profitability**: Reduce team costs by relying more on founder labor in Year 1

### 4.2 Conservative Scenario (Founder-Led, Lean)

If founders handle most operations personally in Year 1 and reduce team costs:

| Metric | Year 1 Lean (Gs.) | Year 1 Lean (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 419,100,000 | $56,635 |
| Costs (salaries reduced 60%) | 410,400,000 | $55,459 |
| **NET PROFIT (Lean)** | **8,700,000** | **$1,176** |

This lean approach reaches near-breakeven in Year 1 but risks burnout and limits growth.

## 9. Tax Planning

### 9.1 Tax Obligations (Paraguay)

| Tax | Rate | Basis | Payment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **IRACIS** (corporate income) | 10% | Net taxable income | Annual |
| **IVA** (value added) | 10% | Product sales, services | Monthly |
| **IRP** (personal income) | 8-10% | Founder/employee income | Annual |
| **IPS** (social security) | 16.5% employer + 9% employee | Payroll | Monthly |
| **Municipal patent** | Variable | Revenue-based | Annual |
| **Withholding on services** | Variable | Contractor payments | Per payment |

### 9.2 Tax Optimization (Legal)

- Register under SIMPLE regime if qualifying (lower rates for small businesses)
- Deduct all legitimate business expenses (events, marketing, inventory)
- Reinvest profits to defer taxable income
- Properly document all import costs as COGS deductions
- Maintain separate accounting for each brand (within single SAS)
- Consider IVA credit for business purchases
