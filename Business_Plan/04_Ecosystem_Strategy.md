## 5. Legal Structure

### 5.1 Recommended Entity

**SAS (Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada)** — Paraguay's simplified corporation:
- Created by Ley 5895/2017
- Single or multiple shareholders
- Minimal capital requirements
- Simpler governance than SA or SRL
- Suitable for startups and small businesses
- Can operate all three brands under one entity

### 5.2 Legal Considerations

| Area | Approach |
|---|---|
| **Business registration** | Register SAS with Ministerio de Industria y Comercio |
| **Tax registration** | RUC (Registro Unico del Contribuyente) with SET |
| **Tax regime** | IRACIS (corporate income tax 10%) or SIMPLE regime if qualifying |
| **IVA** | 10% on products, events may qualify for different treatment |
| **Municipal license** | Patente Comercial with Municipalidad de Asuncion |
| **Trademarks** | Register Fun4Me, Maskarada, Sexpo Paraguay with DINAPI |
| **Privacy** | Comply with Ley 6534/2020 (personal data protection) |
| **Consumer protection** | Comply with Ley 1334/1998 (consumer defense) |
| **E-commerce** | Comply with Ley 4868/2013 (electronic commerce) |
| **Employment** | Comply with Codigo Laboral for employees, IPS contributions |

## 8. Risk Management — Ecosystem Level

### 8.1 Shared Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Regulatory change** (new adult content laws) | Critical | Legal monitoring, industry advocacy, political relationships |
| **Social media deplatforming** (all accounts) | Critical | Email list, Telegram, physical marketing, decentralized channels |
| **Reputation crisis** (safety incident, scandal) | Critical | Crisis communication plan, insurance, transparent response |
| **Key person dependency** | High | Documentation, cross-training, shared knowledge base (this repo) |
| **Conservative organized opposition** | High | Wellness positioning, institutional allies, legal preparedness |
| **Economic downturn** | Medium | Adjust pricing, maintain low-cost event options, essential products |
| **Competitor entry** (well-funded) | Medium | Community moat, brand loyalty, first-mover advantage |

### 8.2 Scenario Planning

**Best case**: 500 ecosystem members by Year 3, profitable across all brands, second city expansion, recognized cultural institution in Asuncion.

**Base case**: 250 members, profitable on events, Fun4Me breaking even, Sexpo annual edition sustaining.

**Worst case**: Regulatory crackdown or major safety incident forces temporary shutdown. Mitigation: maintain community through digital channels, pivot to education-only programming, rebuild under wellness positioning.
