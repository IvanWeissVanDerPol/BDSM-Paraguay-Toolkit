### Fun4Me Store — The Retail Engine
Online adult retail shop and e-commerce platform. Sells wellness products, accessories, and BDSM gear. Handles ticketing for events. The commercial backbone that monetizes the ecosystem year-round.

### Maskarada — The Community Hub
Recurring themed costume events with structured interaction systems (token games). Operates through private channels (Instagram, Telegram, WhatsApp). Builds the loyal community that feeds both retail and expos.

### Sexpo Paraguay — The Awareness Engine
Large-scale expos combining education, entertainment, and cultural disruption. Generates press coverage, attracts newcomers, and normalizes adult wellness discourse in Paraguay. Paraguay's first-ever sex expo (November 2022).
