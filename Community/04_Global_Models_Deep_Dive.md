## 6. Universal Patterns Across All Communities

### 6.1 What Every Successful Community Has

Regardless of country, every thriving kink/adult wellness community has these elements:

1. **Regular munches** — The universal entry point, running weekly or bi-weekly, in vanilla public settings
2. **Education programming** — Workshops, classes, and discussions with emphasis on safety and consent
3. **Structured vetting** — Some process (formal or informal) for evaluating new members
4. **DM/safety team** — Trained event monitors at all play events
5. **Consent framework** — SSC, RACK, PRICK, or custom framework embedded in all programming
6. **Privacy protection** — No-photo rules, no-outing culture, digital security practices
7. **Mentorship pathway** — Way for newcomers to learn from experienced members
8. **Online presence** — FetLife groups, Telegram/Discord, social media (carefully managed)
9. **Community governance** — Some mechanism for community input on rules and direction
10. **Incident response** — Process for handling violations, conflicts, and safety concerns

### 6.2 What Separates Good Communities from Great Ones

| Good | Great |
|---|---|
| Has events | Has a diverse calendar of event types |
| Has rules | Has a culture of consent that goes beyond rules |
| Responds to incidents | Prevents incidents through training and design |
| Has members | Has community roles, leadership pipeline, mentorship |
| Has social media | Has a content strategy with education at its core |
| Runs events | Documents, measures, and improves events systematically |
| Exists year-to-year | Plans in 5-year strategic cycles |
| Has a physical space | The physical space is designed for safety, inclusivity, and diverse programming |
| Attracts newcomers | Onboards newcomers intentionally with buddy systems and orientation |
| Has revenue | Has diversified revenue across products, events, memberships, content |
