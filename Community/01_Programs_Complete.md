# Community Programs & Services — Complete Catalog

## Realistic Offerings for a 2-Person Startup Growing Into a Community

This is the single source of truth for all programs, services, events, and initiatives. Every item is labeled by priority tier and reflects what two founders can actually execute without burning out or pretending they have a team of twenty.

**Ground rules applied to this document:**
- Year 1 = two people, no employees, limited capital, building from zero
- If it requires a dedicated staff member, specialized equipment, or a signed lease, it is not Year 1
- "Monthly" means "when we can" — do not over-commit on frequency
- Revenue estimates assume Asuncion market pricing and realistic attendance
