# Venue & Experience Design — Creating Immersive Spaces

## How to Transform Any Venue into an Unforgettable Experience

---

## 1. Design Philosophy

### 1.1 Core Principles

1. **Safety first, aesthetics second** — Every design choice must pass the safety test before the beauty test
2. **Consent is visible** — The physical environment reinforces consent culture (signage, zones, traffic flow)
3. **Privacy by design** — Layout prevents unwanted visibility between zones
4. **Inclusivity in infrastructure** — Accessibility considerations, gender-neutral spaces, diverse seating
5. **Sensory richness** — Every sense is considered: sight, sound, smell, touch, taste
6. **Transformation** — Even an ordinary venue should feel like entering another world

### 1.2 The Experience Arc

Every event should follow an emotional arc:

```
ARRIVAL          TRANSITION       IMMERSION         PEAK           RESOLUTION      DEPARTURE
   │                 │                │               │                │               │
   ▼                 ▼                ▼               ▼                ▼               ▼
Welcome          Moving from       Fully in the    Emotional/       Coming down,    Gentle exit,
Anticipation     "outside" to      world we've     sensory          aftercare,      carrying the
Excitement       "inside"          created         climax           connection       experience
                                                                                    home
```

### 2.1 Munch / Social Event — Bar or Restaurant

**Requirements**: Table for 15-40, semi-private area, food and drink service

**Layout**:
```

│          RESTAURANT/BAR            │
│                                    │
│   ┌────────┐     ┌────────┐       │
│   │ Table  │     │ Table  │       │

│   └────────┘     └────────┘       │
│                                    │
│   ┌────────┐                      │
│   │ Table  │     ┌────────┐       │
│   │ Group 3│     │  BAR   │       │
│   └────────┘     └────────┘       │
│                                    │
│   HOST POSITION (near entrance)    │
└────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Design elements**:
- Reserved sign or small community marker on tables (discreet — not visible to other diners)
- Host positioned near entrance to greet newcomers
- Tables arranged for conversation (not too loud, not too quiet)
- No special decorations needed — vanilla environment is the point

## 3. Atmosphere Design — The Five Senses

### 3.1 Sight — Lighting

| Zone | Lighting Style | Equipment |
|---|---|---|
| **Entry/check-in** | Well-lit, welcoming, clear visibility | Standard warm lighting |
| **Social/conversation** | Warm, amber, moderate brightness | Table candles (LED), warm Edison bulbs |
| **Dance floor** | Dynamic, colored, pulsing with music | LED par cans, UV/blacklights, moving heads |
| **Play rooms** | Dim, red/amber, adjustable | Red LED strips, dimmer switches, candles (LED) |
| **Shibari area** | Warm but adequate (riggers need to see) | Adjustable warm spotlights |
| **Aftercare/quiet room** | Very soft, warm, like sunset | Low-wattage warm LEDs, fairy lights |
| **Photo area** | Flattering, consistent | Ring light or soft box for photo zone |

**Key lighting rules**:
- NEVER fluorescent or harsh white light at social/play events
- Red light = play zone signal
- Always have emergency lighting (battery backup) visible to DMs
- Light levels should decrease as you move deeper into the venue (entry → play)

### 3.2 Sound — Music & Audio

| Zone | Sound Level | Music Style |
|---|---|---|
| **Entry** | Background (conversation easy) | Ambient, atmospheric, downtempo |
| **Social area** | Moderate (conversation requires proximity) | Deep house, chill electronic, neo-soul |
| **Dance floor** | Loud (designed for dancing) | Dark techno, industrial, bass, DJ sets |
| **Play rooms** | Very low or piped from dance floor (muffled) | Ambient, drone, or muffled main room |
| **Aftercare/quiet** | Very low or silent | Ambient soundscapes, nature sounds, or silence |

**Music curation principles**:
- Build energy gradually (early set: chill → peak: intense → late: winding down)
- NO top-40 pop, reggaeton, or mainstream party music (breaks the immersion)
- Curate playlists that match the theme (see Investigacion/02_Organization_Toolkit/Produccion/01_curaduria_musical.md)
- DJ must understand the event's emotional arc, not just play bangers
- Live percussion, violin, or voice adds organic quality to special events

### 3.3 Smell — Olfactory Design

| Method | Scent Profile | Placement |
|---|---|---|
| **Incense** (quality, not cheap) | Sandalwood, palo santo, nag champa | Entry (transformation signal), social areas |
| **Essential oil diffuser** | Ylang ylang, bergamot, vanilla, cedar | Play rooms, aftercare room |
| **Candles** (soy, beeswax) | Warm, subtle | Tables, bar area, lounge |
| **Fresh flowers** | Natural, elegant | VIP areas, entry |
| **Avoid** | Artificial air freshener, strong chemical smells, nothing at food areas | — |

### 3.4 Touch — Tactile Environment

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Seating textures** | Mix of leather, velvet, soft fabric — no cold plastic chairs |
| **Floor surfaces** | Clean carpeting or mats in play areas, solid floors in social/dance |
| **Temperature** | Cool enough for active dancing, warm enough for undress (AC control is critical) |
| **Blankets** | Soft blankets in aftercare and quiet rooms |
| **Surfaces for play** | Padded furniture, crash mats under suspension points, clean sheets on surfaces |

### 3.5 Taste — Food & Beverage

| Event Type | F&B Strategy |
|---|---|
| **Munch** | Full restaurant menu (own consumption) |
| **Social event** | Bar service, light snacks (olives, nuts, cheese) |
| **Play party** | Water stations (multiple), light snacks, fruit, chocolate |
| **Workshop** | Water, coffee/tea, light snacks |
| **Retreat** | Full catering, communal meals, dietary accommodations |
| **Sexpo** | Multiple food vendors, bar service, water stations |

**Key F&B rules**:
- Water must be available free and abundantly at every event
- Alcohol policy: serve responsibly, cut off visibly intoxicated, no alcohol in play rooms
- Dietary accommodations: label allergens, offer vegetarian/vegan options
- Aftercare food: chocolate, fruit, crackers, tea — comfort items

## 5. Theme Execution Guide

### 5.1 Theme Planning Framework

For each themed event (Maskarada), use this planning template:

1. **Theme name and concept** — Clear, evocative name (e.g., "Noche de Fuego", "Maskarada Noir", "Bosque Oscuro")
2. **Visual reference** — 3-5 reference images that capture the mood (Pinterest board)
3. **Color palette** — Primary and secondary colors for decor, lighting, promotional materials
4. **Music direction** — Genre, tempo, mood progression for the night
5. **Dress code** — What attendees should wear (specific enough to guide, flexible enough to include)
6. **Decor elements** — What physical elements transform the venue
7. **Scent** — What the event should smell like
8. **Food & drink** — How refreshments support the theme (signature cocktail, themed snacks)
9. **Special activities** — Theme-specific programming (performances, interactive elements)
10. **Photo zone** — Themed backdrop for consent-based photography

### 5.2 Theme Ideas Bank

| Theme | Visual | Music | Dress Code | Special Elements |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Maskarada Noir** | Black, silver, candlelight | Dark jazz, trip hop | All black + masks | Candlelit dinner opening, noir photo booth |
| **Noche de Fuego** | Red, orange, flame imagery | Flamenco-fusion, tribal beats | Red/orange/gold | Fire performer, flame-shaped decor, massage candle station |
| **Bosque Oscuro** | Green, brown, nature textures | Organic electronica, forest sounds | Earth tones, nature-inspired | Tree branches decor, moss, fairy lights like fireflies |

## 6. Zones & Traffic Flow

### 6.1 Zone Temperature System

Create a gradual transition from "cool" (vanilla, social) to "hot" (play, intensity):

```
ZONE 1: COLD (Public)          ZONE 2: WARM (Social)           ZONE 3: HOT (Play)
┌─────────────────┐      ┌────────────────────┐      ┌─────────────────────┐
│                 │      │                    │      │                     │
│ Entry, coat     │ ──▶  │ Bar, dance floor,  │ ──▶  │ Play rooms,         │
│ check, welcome, │      │ lounge, social     │      │ shibari space,      │
│ token/wristband │      │ area               │      │ sensory room        │
│                 │      │                    │      │                     │
│ Full lighting   │      │ Moderate lighting   │      │ Dim lighting        │
│ Normal volume   │      │ Music moderate      │      │ Music low/muffled   │
│ No play         │      │ No play             │      │ Play permitted      │
│                 │      │                    │      │ DMs present         │
└─────────────────┘      └────────────────────┘      └─────────────────────┘
                                                              │
                                                              ▼
                                                     ┌─────────────────────┐
                                                     │ ZONE 4: CARE        │
                                                     │                     │
                                                     │ Aftercare lounge,   │
                                                     │ quiet room,         │
                                                     │ blankets, water     │
                                                     │                     │
                                                     │ Softest lighting    │
                                                     │ Very quiet          │
                                                     │ No play             │
                                                     └─────────────────────┘
```

### 6.2 Traffic Flow Principles

1. **One entrance, one exit** (for security and capacity control)
2. **Progressive depth** — Visitors naturally move from social to play areas (not forced)
3. **Escape routes** — Always possible to return to social area without passing through play zones
4. **DM visibility** — DMs positioned at zone transitions and in every play zone
5. **Quiet room access** — Quiet/aftercare room accessible without passing through play zones
6. **Bathroom access** — Toilets accessible from social area (not deep in play zone)
7. **Emergency exits** — Clearly marked, unobstructed, known to all staff

## 7. Safety Infrastructure Design

### 7.1 Safety Stations

Every major event needs:

| Station | Location | Equipment |
|---|---|---|
| **DM base** | Central, visible from most areas | Walkie-talkies, incident forms, spare tokens, safety scissors, flashlights |
| **First aid station** | Near social area (quick access) | Full first aid kit, CPR mask, blankets, water |
| **Aftercare area** | Quiet, separate from play | Blankets, water, snacks, tissues, cushions |
| **Consent/rules board** | Entry area (visible to all) | Printed rules, consent framework, safe words, DM contact |
| **Cleaning station** | Each play room + shared | Disinfectant, wipes, gloves, clean sheets, trash bags |
| **Water station** | Social area + play area | Cooler/bottles, cups (plastic only — no glass) |

### 7.2 Furniture Safety Standards

| Item | Safety Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Suspension points** | Load-rated to 300+ kg, installed by professional, inspected before each event |
| **Suspension frame** | Weight-rated, stable base, no tipping risk, bolted if possible |
| **Crash mats** | Minimum 10cm thick under every suspension point |
| **Furniture padding** | Intact, no exposed nails/screws, stable and non-tipping |
| **Electrical** | All cables taped down, no tripping hazards, no water near electricity |
| **Fire safety** | Real candles ONLY in designated areas with extinguisher present, prefer LED |
| **Emergency lighting** | Battery-powered lights visible in every room |
| **Glass/sharp objects** | No glass (plastic cups only), sharps in designated areas only |

## 9. Post-Event Venue Procedures

### 9.1 Tear-Down Checklist

- [ ] All personal belongings collected (lost and found box)
- [ ] All furniture disinfected and stored
- [ ] All linens/sheets bagged for washing
- [ ] All trash collected and disposed
- [ ] All decor removed and stored
- [ ] Venue walkthrough: check for damage, forgotten items
- [ ] All lights and equipment packed and accounted for
- [ ] Venue returned to pre-event state
- [ ] Venue manager sign-off on condition
- [ ] Equipment transport arranged (vehicle loaded)
- [ ] DM debrief completed before leaving

### 9.2 Equipment Inventory & Maintenance

After every event:
1. Inventory all equipment against master list
2. Note any damaged or missing items
3. Clean and disinfect all furniture and play equipment
4. Recharge batteries (flashlights, LED candles, walkie-talkies)
5. Restock consumables (sheets, cleaning supplies, condoms, wristbands)
6. Store all equipment in secure, climate-controlled location
7. Schedule repairs for damaged items before next event
